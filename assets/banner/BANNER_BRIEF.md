# The phrase shelf

**MOM's Amazon Alexa** is a browser-based guide to spoken commands. The hero presents its catalog as a shelf of paper phrase cards for someone looking up what to say. A TV play button evokes Fire TV, a crescent evokes bedtime lighting and sleep sounds, and a heart bookmark represents browser favorites.

**Primary prompt**
Create a 2:1 editorial paper-cut illustration. Arrange overlapping cream index cards on a deep indigo field above a slim shelf. Show a speech bubble containing a TV with a play triangle, another with a crescent moon, and a coral heart bookmark. Use tactile folds, crisp shadows and saturated periwinkle accents. No text, logos, people or dashboard metrics.

**Alternate prompt**
Draw a flat screen-printed library shelf with three staggered cards using indigo, warm cream, amber and coral. Frame the TV and moon as speech icons. Fold a heart bookmark over the last card. Keep the background opaque and the shapes legible at thumbnail size.

**Text in art** — None. The project title sits below the hero in Markdown.

**Alt text** — Paper phrase cards showing a TV play button, a crescent moon and a heart bookmark on an indigo shelf.

**Placement** — Use `banner.png` above the README title. The same 1280 × 640 composition is saved as `social-preview.png` for manual upload. The independently drawn `banner.svg` is the editable, opaque-background alternative for light and dark pages.

**Refresh** when the phrase catalog, bedtime examples or favorites change enough that one of the three symbols loses its meaning.
